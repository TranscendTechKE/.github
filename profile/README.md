# TranscendTech

### Silicon in the Savannah

**Community. Education. Technology. Innovation.**

TranscendTech is an emerging community technology, education and innovation initiative rooted in Samburu County, Kenya.

We bring together developers, educators, learners, makers, researchers and community innovators to build practical skills, develop useful tools and explore technology-driven solutions to local challenges.

**Build Together. Learn Together. Create.**

[Visit our website](https://transcendtech.netlify.app)



## Our Vision

A future where communities are empowered to learn, create and use technology to solve their own challenges and shape their own opportunities.

## Our Mission

To empower communities through education, technology, practical innovation and collaboration by developing people, skills, tools and spaces that transform local challenges into sustainable opportunities.



## What We Work On

Our work is organized around seven interconnected programme areas.

### Education & EdTech

Developing curriculum-aware educational tools, teacher resources and practical approaches to digital learning.

### Makerspace, STEM & STEAM

Exploring hands-on learning through electronics, Arduino, robotics, prototyping, fabrication and practical science.

### Digital Inclusion & Community Technology

Supporting digital skills, accessible technology and community-oriented digital systems.

### Community Data, AI & Local-Language Technology

Exploring responsible AI, language technology, community datasets and tools that can support local knowledge.

### Climate & Resilience Technology

Investigating practical technology for water, solar energy, environmental monitoring and climate resilience.

### Youth Skills, Mentorship & Innovation

Creating opportunities for collaborative learning, project-based development, mentorship and technical contribution.

### Research, Knowledge & Evidence

Documenting what we learn, evaluating our work and building evidence to inform better decisions.



## What We're Building

Our projects span education technology, open-source software, language technology, maker education and community innovation.

Examples include:

* **CBE Lesson Builder** — a curriculum-aware lesson-planning tool under development.
* **CBE Teacher Network** — a proposed broader platform for teacher resources and collaboration.
* **TranscendTech Developer & Maker Network** — a community platform for technical collaboration and project-based learning.
* **Samburu Language Technology** — ongoing work exploring computational language resources and speech technology.
* **Maralal Makerspace** — a longer-term vision for practical, collaborative STEM and maker learning.
* **CommunityOS and Pastoralist Data Intelligence** — concepts exploring community information systems and locally relevant data tools.

Project maturity varies. We aim to distinguish working software, active development, experiments, proposals and long-term ideas clearly.



## Contribute

You do not have to be an expert to contribute.

We welcome people interested in:

* Software and web development
* Data analysis and responsible AI
* Education and curriculum resources
* Electronics, Arduino and robotics
* Samburu language and local knowledge
* Research and technical documentation
* UI/UX design and communication
* Testing, mentoring and community engagement

You can contribute by improving documentation, testing a tool, reporting an issue, sharing knowledge, developing a feature or helping another learner.

**Hatujui yote. We learn from one another.**

Please check each project's README for its current status and contribution instructions before getting started.



## Our Principles

* **Community ownership** — involve communities in defining problems and shaping solutions.
* **Learning by doing** — build skills through practical work.
* **Open collaboration** — share knowledge and welcome constructive contributions.
* **Integrity and accountability** — describe our work and results honestly.
* **Responsible technology** — consider privacy, security, accessibility and community benefit.
* **Local knowledge matters** — build with communities, not merely for them.
